Electricity safety and saving tips

A few practical habits can help reduce waste and keep people safer around electricity.

🔌

Unplug carefully

Switch off equipment before unplugging it. Pull the plug, not the cable.

💡

Save energy

Turn off lights and devices when they are not needed, and choose efficient bulbs.

⚠️

Avoid exposed wires

Keep away from damaged wires and report them to the relevant authority.

🧰

Use qualified help

Ask a qualified electrician to inspect faults or carry out electrical repairs.

🌧️

Be careful around water

Do not touch electrical appliances or switches with wet hands.

📱

Keep contacts handy

Use verified official complaint and emergency channels for your area.

If there is an immediate electrical danger, keep a safe distance and contact the relevant local emergency or utility service. Do not attempt repairs yourself.
