Safety first

Emergency information

Use verified local emergency services and official utility channels when you need help.

Electrical hazard: Stay away from fallen or exposed wires. Do not touch a person or object that may be in contact with a live wire. Contact trained emergency responders and the electricity provider.

Electricity fault or outage

Contact your electricity provider through the official website, app, or published complaint number.
