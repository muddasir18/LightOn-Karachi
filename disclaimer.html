LightOn Karachi is a sample static website created to demonstrate web development using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It is not an official utility service and is not affiliated with any electricity provider.

Accuracy and availability

Area labels, schedule rows, and other example information may be incomplete or fictional. They must not be relied on for current load-shedding times, restoration estimates, emergency response, or other time-sensitive decisions.

External websites

Links to external websites are provided as convenience links. Review the destination and its information independently.

Forms and privacy

Contact and feedback forms in this version do not transmit or store submissions. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

Updates

If this project is expanded into a public information service, all operational data should be verified against reliable official sources and display when it was last updated.
