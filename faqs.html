Frequently asked questions

FAQs

Select a question to read its answer.

Does this website show live load-shedding timings?

No. This version is a static educational demo. It does not connect to a live electricity data source.

Are the area listings official?

No. Area names and zone labels are examples used to demonstrate the website features.

Where can I verify electricity updates?

Use your electricity provider's official website, application, or verified announcements.

Can I search an area?

Yes. Open the Area Search page and type a neighbourhood name or sample zone.

Does the contact form send messages?

No. The form demonstrates browser-side validation only. It does not send or store messages online.

Can I add more areas?

Yes. Add the new area to the sample data in app.js and update the area cards in areas.html.
